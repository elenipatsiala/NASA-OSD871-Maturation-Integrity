01 · The harder question

Can an organoid look transcriptionally mature without becoming a better dopaminergic model?

The Build reproduced the selected published Ground-versus-LEO marker directions. The Iterate raises the bar by keeping maturation, dopaminergic identity, progenitor state and stress as separate biological dimensions.

Build asked

Can the selected published LEO-associated transcriptional pattern be reproduced?

Iterate asks

If the state looks more mature, is dopaminergic fidelity actually preserved — and does the interpretation survive robustness tests?

Control + microglia breaks the simple story.

08 · Cross-study triangulation

Why progenitor ↓ is not proof of useful maturation.

NASA OSD-871

Dopaminergic organoids · actual ISS / LEO exposure · bulk RNA-seq · overall maturation-associated pattern with subgroup heterogeneity.

Park et al. 2026

Cerebral organoids · 3D clinostat · targeted gene/protein/neurochemical readouts · progenitor-marker loss did not equal straightforward beneficial maturation.

09 · Qualified conclusion

The overall pattern is robust. The biological interpretation is conditional.

Supported

Overall LEO-associated transcription shifted toward higher maturation, higher dopaminergic identity and lower progenitor/cell-cycle and stress scores, with overall directions robust to single-gene removal.

Not supported

Universal improvement, functional maturation, microgravity-specific causality, a better Parkinson’s model or a validated biomarker.
