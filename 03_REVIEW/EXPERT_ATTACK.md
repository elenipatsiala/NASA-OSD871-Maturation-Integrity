# ITERATE — Expert Attack

## Purpose

This document deliberately challenges the strongest interpretation of the Iterate results.

The goal is not to defend the hypothesis, but to identify what evidence could falsify or substantially weaken the claim that the LEO-associated transcriptional pattern represents useful dopaminergic maturation.

---

## Current Qualified Claim

OSD-871 shows an overall LEO-associated transcriptional shift toward:

- higher neuronal maturation,
- higher dopaminergic identity,
- lower progenitor / cell-cycle state,
- and lower cellular stress.

The overall directions are robust to removal of individual genes.

However, subgroup heterogeneity shows that maturation and dopaminergic fidelity can diverge.

---

## Attack 1 — Are the program scores biologically valid?

The program scores are literature-derived exploratory summaries.

They have not been experimentally validated as biomarkers of neuronal maturation or dopaminergic fidelity.

Equal weighting of genes may oversimplify differences in biological importance.

### What would weaken the framework?

If alternative reasonable literature-derived gene sets produce substantially different Ground-versus-LEO directions.

### Required test

Run alternative-marker sensitivity analyses during the Create stage.

---

## Attack 2 — Does transcription equal biological function?

No.

The analysis measures RNA-level expression.

Higher maturation-associated transcription does not demonstrate:

- mature electrophysiology,
- synaptic function,
- dopamine synthesis or release,
- mature protein abundance,
- neuronal survival,
- or functional Parkinson's disease phenotypes.

### What would falsify “useful maturation”?

If LEO samples show maturation-associated transcription but impaired:

- dopamine release,
- electrophysiology,
- neuronal connectivity,
- or dopaminergic protein expression.

---

## Attack 3 — Could cell composition explain the scores?

Yes.

Bulk RNA-seq cannot distinguish:

1. changes occurring within the same cells,
2. changes in the proportion of neuronal subtypes,
3. altered progenitor abundance,
4. altered astroglial abundance,
5. or altered microglial contribution.

### Required future evidence

Single-cell RNA-seq, spatial profiling, cell-type deconvolution or validated cell-composition measurements.

---

## Attack 4 — Is the dopaminergic identity result universal?

No.

Control + microglia showed:

- increased Maturation,
- decreased Dopaminergic Identity.

This decrease persisted across all leave-one-gene-out identity analyses.

### Implication

Maturation-associated transcription can occur without preservation of dopaminergic identity.

This directly argues against a universal “LEO improves dopaminergic maturation” claim.

---

## Attack 5 — Is the stress result universally robust?

No.

The Control without microglia subgroup had only a small primary Stress decrease.

Removal of HSPA5, HSP90B1, DDIT3 or ATF4 reversed the direction.

### Implication

Stress reduction is not a robust conclusion in every subgroup.

---

## Attack 6 — Could donor / cell-line effects explain the biology?

Yes.

OSD-871 contains one Control donor-specific line and one PD donor-specific line.

Disease status is therefore confounded with donor and cell line.

### Consequence

The analysis cannot establish general PD-specific versus Control-specific LEO responses.

The most defensible interpretation remains within-cell-line Ground-versus-LEO comparison.

---

## Attack 7 — Can the effect be attributed specifically to microgravity?

No.

ISS / LEO exposure includes multiple environmental variables.

The analysis cannot isolate microgravity from:

- radiation,
- launch / return stress,
- vibration,
- altered gas exchange,
- hardware effects,
- or other spaceflight-associated conditions.

### Correct terminology

Use:

“LEO-associated” or “spaceflight-associated”

rather than:

“microgravity caused”.

---

## Attack 8 — Does cross-study evidence validate OSD-871?

No.

The Park et al. 2026 clinostat study uses:

- cerebral organoids,
- simulated microgravity,
- different biological readouts,
- and a different experimental environment.

It provides triangulation, not replication.

### Why it still matters

It demonstrates that:

progenitor / stemness markers decreasing
does not necessarily imply
beneficial neuronal maturation.

---

## Strongest Falsification Experiment

A strong future test would measure, in matched Ground and LEO dopaminergic organoids:

1. transcriptomic maturation,
2. ventral-midbrain dopaminergic identity,
3. TH / DAT / VMAT2 protein expression,
4. dopamine production and release,
5. electrophysiological maturation,
6. cellular composition,
7. stress-state markers.

The useful-maturation hypothesis would be strongly weakened if transcriptional maturation increased while dopaminergic function or identity declined.

---

## Expert Questions

1. Which evidence would you require before calling this “dopaminergic maturation” rather than a transcriptional state shift?

2. Are the selected maturation and dopaminergic-identity gene programs biologically defensible?

3. Which alternative explanation is currently most plausible:
   within-cell maturation, cell-composition change, stress adaptation, or lineage shift?

4. What single additional measurement would most strongly distinguish useful maturation from misleading maturation-associated transcription?

5. Does the Control + microglia counterexample meaningfully weaken the overall useful-maturation interpretation?

---

## Current Verdict

The Iterate does not establish that LEO produces better Parkinson's disease models.

It supports a narrower conclusion:

**LEO-associated transcriptional maturation and dopaminergic fidelity are related but separable biological dimensions.**

The next stage should therefore preserve those dimensions rather than compressing them into a single “maturity” score.
