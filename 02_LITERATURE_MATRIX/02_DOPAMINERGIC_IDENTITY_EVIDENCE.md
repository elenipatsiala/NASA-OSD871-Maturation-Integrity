# Dopaminergic Identity / Integrity Program — Evidence v1

## Purpose

This program is intended to capture ventral-midbrain dopaminergic identity and maintenance rather than general neuronal maturation alone.

The program deliberately combines:
1. ventral-midbrain lineage identity,
2. dopamine synthesis / handling machinery,
3. an A9-like dopaminergic fidelity marker.

## Important interpretation rule

No single gene will be treated as sufficient evidence of dopaminergic fidelity.

In particular, increased TH alone will not be interpreted as preserved ventral-midbrain dopaminergic identity.

The integrity interpretation will depend on whether multiple regional, lineage and dopamine-handling genes behave coherently.

## Sensitivity candidates

The following genes may be evaluated later in a sensitivity analysis but are not part of the core v1 score:

- ALDH1A1 — A9/substantia-nigra-enriched marker but subtype-restricted
- SOX6 — relevant to substantia nigra dopaminergic subtype specification
- OTX2 — regional identity marker but also expressed earlier in development
- CALB1 — associated more strongly with other dopaminergic subtypes and therefore not a generic integrity marker
